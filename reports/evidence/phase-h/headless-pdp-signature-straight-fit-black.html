How to measure

Chest / Width
Measure across the garment flat one inch below the armhole.
Length
Measure from the highest point of the shoulder straight down to the bottom hem.
Waist
Measure across the top of the waistband relaxed to stretched in inches.
Hip Round
Measure flat across the widest point of the hips and double it.
